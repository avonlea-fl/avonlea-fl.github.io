Thank you for representing a buyer or seller in Avonlea at Smith Lake. This page collects the details agents most often ask about. Please share it with your clients.

### Community Overview

* **Location:** Marion County, Florida, adjacent to Smith Lake
* **Home sites:** Two to over eight acres, averaging five acres
* **Lake access:** Private lake access via two common areas with a dock, ramp, and sandy beach
* **Horses:** Equestrian-friendly, as specified in the deed restrictions
* **Utilities:** Underground electrical service and street lights throughout
